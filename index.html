Fitts' Law Experiment

This experiment investigates the relationship between target distance, target size and mouse movement time.

Instructions

  1. You will first complete 10 practice trials.
  2. Practice trials are not included in the final dataset.
  3. You will then complete 100 experimental trials.
  4. At the beginning of each trial, move your mouse into the experiment area.
  5. A blue square will appear at a specified distance from your current mouse position.
  6. Move to the blue square as quickly and accurately as possible.
  7. Clicking outside the blue square counts as an error.
  8. Try to move as directly as possible toward the target.

Please use a laptop or desktop with a mouse or trackpad.

Please do not refresh or close the page during the experiment.
