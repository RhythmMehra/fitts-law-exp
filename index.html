Fitts' Law Experiment

This experiment investigates how target size and target distance affect mouse movement time.

Instructions

  1. You will first complete 10 practice trials.
  2. The practice trials will not be recorded.
  3. You will then complete 100 experimental trials.
  4. A small grey dot will indicate where to position your mouse.
  5. Move your mouse onto the grey dot.
  6. Once the blue target appears, move to it as quickly and accurately as possible.
  7. Clicking outside the blue target counts as an error.
  8. Try to move directly toward the target.

Please do not refresh or close the page during the experiment.
