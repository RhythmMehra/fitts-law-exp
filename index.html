Fitts' Law Experiment

This experiment investigates the relationship between target size, target distance and mouse movement time.

Instructions

  1. You will first complete 10 practice trials.
  2. Practice trials will not be included in the final dataset.
  3. You will then complete 100 experimental trials.
  4. A grey dot will indicate the starting position of your mouse.
  5. Move your mouse onto the grey dot.
  6. A blue square will then appear.
  7. Click the blue square as quickly and accurately as possible.
  8. Clicking outside the square counts as an error.
  9. Try to move directly toward the target.

Please do not refresh or close the browser during the experiment.
