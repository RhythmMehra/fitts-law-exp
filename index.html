Fitts' Law Experiment

This experiment investigates the relationship between target distance, target size and mouse movement time.

Instructions

  1. Enter your name below.
  2. You will first complete 3 practice trials.
  3. After practice, click the red START EXPERIMENT button.
  4. A red dot will appear at the beginning of every trial.
  5. Move your mouse onto the red dot.
  6. A blue square will then appear.
  7. Move to the blue square as quickly and accurately as possible and click it.
  8. Clicking outside the blue square counts as an error.
  9. A new red starting dot will automatically appear for the next trial.

Please use a laptop or desktop with a mouse or trackpad.

Please do not refresh or close the page during the experiment.
